Executive Business Insights & Key Findings

Analytical Synthesis

1. Revenue Trajectory & Peak +77.7% H2 Surge

Total revenue reached ₹72,97,100.50. Revenue slowed through Q1 to reach its annual lowest point in April (₹5,88,288.20 / 113 txns), before staging a major sustained recovery through summer and culminating in an all-time peak in September (₹10,45,585.80 / 147 txns).

2. Category Concentration 57.78% Electronics

Electronics is the primary revenue powerhouse, generating ₹42,15,977.10 (57.78% of store revenue from just 20.4% of orders). Home & Kitchen forms the second revenue pillar at ₹12.91L (17.69%). While Books and Beauty sustain high transaction volumes, their lower basket values limit gross contribution.

3. Repeat Purchase Polarisation 2.18x Retention Gap

Overall repeat transaction rate stands at 34.25% (411/1200). Customer retention is highly segmented: Premium buyers lead at 40.62%, and Mid-Tier at 37.60%. However, Budget customers lag drastically at only 18.64%, representing substantial untapped retention upside.

4. Delivery Latency & Ratings r = -0.669 Correlation

A strong negative Pearson correlation (r = -0.669, Spearman: -0.560) proves delivery speed drives customer satisfaction. Orders delivered in 1–2 days achieve an outstanding 4.72 / 5.0 rating. Once delivery exceeds 6 days (7+ days), customer satisfaction plummets to 2.53 / 5.0.

Strategic Action Plan & Management Recommendations

Logistics SLA
Enforce Strict 4-Day Delivery Threshold: 87% of orders are currently delivered under 4 days with satisfaction > 4.36/5. Restructure 3PL partner contracts and regional fulfillment routing to eliminate 7+ day delays where ratings collapse to 2.53.
Quality Control
Electronics Pre-Dispatch Verification: Electronics exhibits the highest return rate at 10.61% (26 returned units). Implement mandatory pre-dispatch testing and shock-proof tamper packaging to reduce return drag and reverse logistics expense.
Retention Engine
Targeted Budget-Segment Onboarding: Bridge the repeat rate gap between Budget (18.64%) and Premium (40.62%) by issuing automatic ₹150–₹250 follow-up vouchers on first purchases and launching an accessible milestone loyalty program.

Standard viva evaluation answers and core mathematical methodologies

Q1. What is the dataset and its key characteristics?
The dataset is UrbanMart_Transactions_1200_Rows.xlsx containing 1,200 transaction records, 1,074 unique customers, 6 product categories, 3 customer segments, and 8 Indian cities between 01 Jan 2024 and 30 Sep 2024. The sheet contains 0 missing values and 0 duplicate rows. (330 rows were historical; 870 rows were synthesized for academic modeling).
Q2. What are the 4 core Verified KPIs?
1. Total Revenue: ₹72,97,100.50
2. Total Transactions: 1,200
3. Top Product Category: Electronics (₹42,15,977.10 / 57.78% share)
4. Highest Revenue Month: September (₹10,45,585.80 / 147 transactions)
Q3. What is the relationship between delivery duration and customer rating?
The Pearson correlation is -0.669 (Spearman rank: -0.560). This indicates a strong negative linear association: as delivery days increase from 1–2 days (avg rating 4.72) to 7+ days, the customer satisfaction rating drops sharply to 2.53 out of 5.0.
Q4. How does repeat-purchase behavior differ across segments?
Repeat-purchase transaction rate is highest in Premium (40.62%), followed by Mid-Tier (37.60%), and lowest in Budget (18.64%). Overall repeat rate is 34.25% (411 out of 1,200 transactions). Note: this is a transaction-level flag rather than a lifetime customer cohort retention rate.
Q5. Which category has the highest return rate and what are the strategic fixes?
Electronics has the highest return rate at 10.61% (26 of 245 orders), compared to the overall average of 7.83%. Strategic fixes include stricter pre-dispatch electronic quality inspections, improved shock-proof protective packaging, and clearer product technical specs to eliminate buyer expectation mismatches.
